Guide: how to write good solutions

When writing answers: Data is different for everyone, so give the step by step of the method - what prompts to run, what to save, in what order - not the direct answer.

When reviewing answers: Say exactly what error, or what exactly doesn't work? Did you follow all the steps but somehow the answer is different? Paste the exact error message. "doesn't work" is not enough answer.
